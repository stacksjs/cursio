# Cursio

Cursio is a hosted platform where a business runs its own online school: it publishes courses, sells
access to them, and teaches learners alone or in cohorts.

## Language

### Tenancy and people

**Academy**:
One customer's school on Cursio, with its own address, branding, catalog and learners.
_Avoid_: School, site, tenant, workspace, organization

**Owner**:
The person who holds an Academy's account and its billing.
_Avoid_: Admin (an Admin runs the Academy but does not own it)

**Instructor**:
A member of an Academy who authors Courses and teaches them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, author, creator, coach

**Learner**:
A person who takes Courses in an Academy.
_Avoid_: Student, user, member, customer

### Catalog

**Course**:
A structured body of teaching on one subject that a Learner can enroll in and complete.
_Avoid_: Class, program, product

**Section**:
An ordered group of Lessons inside a Course.
_Avoid_: Module, chapter, unit

**Lesson**:
The smallest thing a Learner opens and completes: a video, a reading, a Quiz, an Assignment or a
Live Session.
_Avoid_: Topic, step, item, content

**Bundle**:
Several Courses sold together as one purchase.
_Avoid_: Package, collection

**Learning Path**:
An ordered sequence of Courses that leads to one outcome.
_Avoid_: Track, roadmap, curriculum

### Access

**Offer**:
One way to get into a Course, Bundle or Membership, with its price and terms.
_Avoid_: Plan, price, product, pricing tier

**Enrollment**:
A Learner's right to take one Course, with when it starts and when it ends.
_Avoid_: Registration, subscription, access, purchase

**Membership**:
A recurring Offer that enrolls the Learner in a set of Courses for as long as it stays paid.
_Avoid_: Subscription (that is the payment), plan

**Drip Rule**:
A condition that decides when a Lesson opens for an enrolled Learner.
_Avoid_: Schedule, unlock, release

### Progress and assessment

**Progress**:
How far a Learner has got through a Lesson or a Course.
_Avoid_: Completion (that is the end state), status

**Quiz**:
A set of Questions a Learner answers and that is scored automatically where it can be.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, assessment

**Attempt**:
One sitting of a Quiz by one Learner.
_Avoid_: Submission (that belongs to Assignments), try, result

**Assignment**:
Work a Learner hands in for an Instructor or peers to review.
_Avoid_: Homework, project, task

**Submission**:
What a Learner hands in for an Assignment.
_Avoid_: Attempt, upload, answer

**Certificate**:
A verifiable record that a Learner completed a Course.
_Avoid_: Diploma, credential, badge (a Badge is a smaller award)

### Cohorts and community

**Cohort**:
A group of Learners who take a Course together between a start date and an end date.
_Avoid_: Group (reserved for company training), class, batch, intake

**Live Session**:
A scheduled real-time meeting that belongs to a Cohort or a Course.
_Avoid_: Webinar, call, event, class

**Space**:
A place in an Academy where Learners and Instructors hold Threads.
_Avoid_: Forum, channel, community, board

**Thread**:
One discussion in a Space, opened by a post and continued by Replies.
_Avoid_: Topic (LearnDash uses it for a lesson part), post, discussion
